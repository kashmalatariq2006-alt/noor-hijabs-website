About Noor Hijabs

Our story

Noor Hijabs is an online boutique created by The Modest Minds. It shows how a small hijab seller could present fabrics, prices and orders on one clear website.

Our values

  • Honest descriptions of every fabric
  • Simple and respectful service
  • Comfort for everyday wear
  • Clear prices with no surprises

Care tips

  • Wash gently by hand or on a delicate cycle
  • Use mild detergent
  • Do not wring or twist the fabric
  • Dry flat in the shade
  • Iron on low heat, using a cloth for silk and chiffon
