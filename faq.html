Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Open the Order page, fill in the form and send your inquiry. The shop confirms by WhatsApp.

Which fabric is best for summer?

Georgette and chiffon are light and airy, so they suit hot weather.

How long does delivery take?

Sample answer: 3 to 5 working days within Pakistan.

Can I return or exchange a product?

Sample answer: unused items can be exchanged within 7 days.

How can I pay?

Sample answer: cash on delivery or bank transfer, arranged by WhatsApp. There is no online payment on this website.
